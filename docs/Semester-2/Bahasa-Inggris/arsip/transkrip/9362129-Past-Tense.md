## Ringkasan detail

Video sesi 6 grammar (sekitar 29 menit) ini membahas *past tense*. Sama seperti present tense, past tense punya empat bentuk: *simple, continuous, perfect, perfect continuous*. Tujuan: mahasiswa memahami konstruksi dan penggunaan past tense, serta mampu menerapkannya secara tertulis dan lisan. Dosen menjelaskan tiap bentuk dengan fungsi, contoh, pola positif/negatif/tanya, dan ditutup dengan rumus ringkas.

- [00:00–03:15] **Simple past tense** = cara paling umum membicarakan kejadian atau keadaan (*state*) yang sudah selesai di masa lalu.
  - Kejadian tunggal: "I stopped at a zebra crossing." ; "We carried on with the test."
  - Kejadian berulang di masa lalu: "We played tennis every day in August."
  - *State* (situasi tanpa aksi): "We stayed at my grandparents' house last summer."
- [03:15–04:20] **Pola simple past**.
  - Positif: S + V2. Contoh: "Julia made a sponge cake." ; "He went to the hill station." (*made, went* = irregular verbs; regular verbs ditambah -ed).
  - Negatif: S + did not (didn't) + V1: "Julia did not make a sponge cake." ; "He did not go to the hill station." (yang berubah ke bentuk past adalah *did*, bukan kata kerjanya).
  - Tanya: Did + S + V1: "Did Julia make a sponge cake?" ; "Did he go to the hill station?"
- [04:20–10:37] **Past continuous tense** = aksi yang sedang berlangsung di masa lalu (awal/akhirnya tidak disebut).
  - Berlangsung sebelum dan sesudah aksi lain: "The children were doing their homework when I got home."
  - Berlangsung pada waktu tertentu: "It was eight o'clock. I was writing a letter." ; "He was driving a car at 5 p.m. yesterday."
  - Menunjukkan sesuatu berlangsung beberapa waktu: "My head was aching. Everyone was shouting." (dosen menekankan pentingnya konteks seluruh percakapan)
  - Kejadian yang terjadi berulang: "I was practicing every day, three times a day." ; "They were meeting secretly after school." ; "They were always quarreling."
  - Dengan kata kerja perubahan/pertumbuhan: "The children were growing up quickly." ; "Her English was improving." ; "My hair was going gray." ; "The town was changing quickly."
- [10:37–12:47] **Pola past continuous**: S + was/were + V-ing (was untuk I/he/she/it, were untuk you/we/they). Contoh: "He was traveling to Venice." ; "She was eating a burger." ; "were not singing". Negatif: not setelah was/were. Tanya: was/were pindah ke depan subjek.
- [12:47–19:06] **Past perfect tense**.
  - Fungsi 1: membicarakan waktu sampai titik tertentu di masa lalu. "She had published her first poem by the time she was eight." ; "We had finished all the water before we were halfway up the mountain." ; "Had the parcel arrived when you called yesterday?"
  - Fungsi 2: menunjukkan urutan dua kejadian masa lalu: past perfect untuk aksi yang lebih dulu, past simple untuk yang kemudian. "When the police arrived, the thief had escaped." = "The thief had escaped when the police arrived." (urutan klausa boleh dibalik; pakai koma bila *when* di depan, tanpa koma bila *when* di belakang).
  - *before* + past perfect untuk aksi yang tidak sempat selesai: "They left before I'd spoken to them." ; "Sadly, the author died before he'd finished the series."
- [19:06–21:14] **Adverbs yang sering dipakai dengan past perfect**: already, still, just, ever, never. Contoh: "I called this office, but he'd already left." ; "It still hadn't rained at the beginning of May." ; "I went to visit her when she'd just moved to Berlin." ; "It was the most beautiful photo I had ever seen." ; "Had you ever visited London when you moved there?" ; "I'd never met anyone from California before I met Jim."
- [21:14–22:22] **Pola past perfect**: S + had + V3. Contoh: "The players had lost heart before the match finished." ; "Chris had completed the assignment before he went to school." Negatif: not di antara had dan V3. Tanya: had pindah ke depan. (Untuk continuous yang dipindah adalah was/were; untuk perfect adalah had.)
- [22:22–26:35] **Past perfect continuous tense** = aksi yang dimulai di masa lalu dan berlangsung beberapa waktu sebelum titik tertentu di masa lalu; menekankan durasi (for, since, how long). Dosen menyebutkan bentuk ini rumit dan belum wajib dikuasai pemula/general English, tetapi baik diketahui.
  - "I had been learning French for two years before I went to college."
  - "He had been drinking milk out of the carton when Mom walked into the kitchen (last night)."
  - "I had been working at the company for five years when I got the promotion."
- [26:35–28:44] **Rumus ringkas past tense**.
  - Simple: S + V2 (regular: -ed; irregular, mis. go - went - gone).
  - Continuous: S + was/were + V-ing.
  - Perfect: S + had + V3.
  - Perfect continuous: S + had been + V-ing.
  - Penutup dan referensi.

**Vocabulary penting:** *irregular verbs* (kata kerja tidak beraturan), *regular verbs*, *state* (keadaan), *repeated action*, *ongoing*, *duration*, *escape* (kabur), *carton*, *promotion*.

**Poin penting untuk ujian:**
- Empat rumus past tense: V2 / was-were + V-ing / had + V3 / had been + V-ing.
- Simple past negatif/tanya memakai *did* + V1 (kata kerja kembali ke bentuk pertama).
- Past continuous dipakai untuk aksi yang sedang berlangsung saat aksi lain terjadi ("were doing homework when I got home").
- Past perfect untuk aksi yang lebih dulu: past perfect = lebih awal, past simple = lebih kemudian; signal words already, still, just, ever, never, before, by the time.
- *When* di depan klausa memakai koma; di belakang tanpa koma.
- Past perfect continuous menekankan durasi dan tergolong tingkat lanjut.
