## Ringkasan detail

Video sesi 4 bertema *Health* (sekitar 24 menit) ini bertujuan membuat mahasiswa menguasai makna kosa kata kesehatan dan memakainya sesuai konteks, lisan maupun tulisan. Dosen membagi masalah kesehatan menjadi dua: *illness* (penyakit) dan *injuries* (cedera). Isi: penyakit ringan beserta gejala dan penyebab, perbedaan *ache*, *pain*, *hurt*, *painful*, penyakit serius, lima cedera umum dengan struktur kalimatnya, kosa kata perawatan di rumah sakit, serta perbedaan *wound* dan *injury*.

- [00:01–01:08] Pembukaan dan tujuan. Dua masalah kesehatan: *illness* dan *injuries*.
- [01:08–02:11] **Common problems of illness**: "She's sneezing." (bersin), "She's coughing." (batuk), "She's got a sore throat." (radang tenggorokan), "She's blowing her nose." (membuang ingus), "She's got a temperature." (demam).
- [02:11–05:15] **Tabel penyakit: What's the matter? / symptoms / cause**.
  - "I've got a cold." (pilek): gejala sneezing, sore throat, cough; penyebab *virus*.
  - "I've got the flu." (flu, lebih serius dari cold): gejala sama ditambah *high temperature*; penyebab virus.
  - "I've got diarrhea.": gejala "I keep going to the toilet."; penyebab makanan tidak bersih atau virus.
  - "I feel sick." (mual): gejala "I want to vomit."; penyebab food, illness, atau trip.
  - Pengobatan: pergi ke *doctor* atau *chemist* (apoteker/apotek). Dokter memberi *prescription* (resep) yang ditebus di chemist.
- [05:15–07:19] **Ache vs pain**.
  - *ache* (noun) untuk toothache, stomachache, backache, earache, headache: "I got a terrible headache." ; "I often get backache."
  - *pain* untuk bagian tubuh lain: "I woke up with a terrible pain in my chest." ; "I get a pain in my leg when I run."
- [07:19–09:23] **Ache dan hurt sebagai verb**.
  - *ache* (verb) = sakit yang berlangsung lama tetapi tidak terlalu kuat: "By the end of the day, my feet were aching."
  - *hurt* = sakit yang lebih kuat atau mendadak: "My throat hurts when I speak." ; pertanyaan: "Where does it hurt?"
  - Adjective yang paling umum: *painful*. Dialog: "Did it hurt when you had the filling?" / "It was quite painful when she gave me the injection." (di transkrip tertulis "feelings", salah dengar; yang benar *filling* = tambalan gigi saat dokter gigi menutup lubang/cavity).
- [10:25–11:28] **Serious illness**: "Smoking is a major cause of lung cancer." ; "He had a heart attack and died almost immediately." ; "Hepatitis is a disease affecting the liver." ; *suffer from* untuk menyatakan seseorang menderita penyakit: "Many people suffer from asthma."
- [11:28–15:32] **Common injuries** (*injury* = damage to part of your body). Tabel problem / how did it happen / result / solution:
  - "She cut her finger." : using a knife; it's bleeding; solusi *plaster*.
  - "He twisted his ankle." : running for a bus; he can't walk; solusi *bandage*.
  - "She's got a bruise." : she hit her ankle when she fell over; swollen, black and blue; solusi *ice pack*.
  - "He broke his arm." : he fell off his bike; he can't use it; solusi *sling*.
  - "She burned her hand." : on the kettle; it's very painful; solusi *cream*.
- [15:32–18:37] **Struktur kalimat Inggris vs Indonesia**. Kalimat seperti "She cut her finger" berpola S + V + O dan secara literal terdengar seolah subjek sengaja melakukannya ("Dia memotong jarinya"), padahal kejadiannya tidak disengaja; dalam bahasa Indonesia lebih natural "jarinya terpotong". Begitu juga "He twisted his ankle", "He broke his arm", "She burned her hand".
- [18:37–19:43] *hurt* untuk cedera: "I hurt my back in the garden." ; "She hurt herself when she fell over." ; "I hit my leg against the table and it really hurts."
- [19:43–21:44] **Hospital treatment**. Cerita John: "John fell out of the window and hit his head on the ground. His wife called an ambulance, but John was still unconscious when it arrived. He was rushed to hospital and when he came around, he didn't know where he was." Vocabulary: *fell out of*, *ambulance*, *unconscious* (pingsan), *rushed to hospital* (dilarikan ke rumah sakit), *come around* (sadar kembali). Cerita kedua: "Two players jumped for the ball and bumped into each other. One cut his head and had to go to hospital. He needed ten stitches." *bump into* = bertabrakan tidak sengaja; *stitches* = jahitan.
- [21:44–23:52] **Wound vs injury**. Keduanya kerusakan pada tubuh, tetapi *wound* biasanya disebabkan senjata (gun, knife) dalam perkelahian atau perang. Kata kerja: *wound* dan *injure*. Contoh: "He was seriously injured in a car crash." ; "Two of the soldiers were wounded." ; "Someone shot him with a gun." ; "He got into a fight and he was beaten up."
- [23:52–akhir] Dosen menyediakan tautan YouTube untuk berlatih *English for health / English for hospital*.

**Poin penting untuk ujian:**
- Illness vs injury; gejala umum: sneezing, coughing, sore throat, temperature.
- Cold vs flu (flu = gejala cold + high temperature); *suffer from* + penyakit.
- *ache* untuk tooth, stomach, back, ear, head; *pain* untuk bagian tubuh lain; *hurt* lebih kuat/mendadak; *painful* = adjective.
- Lima injury: cut, twist (ankle), bruise, break (arm), burn, beserta solusi (plaster, bandage, ice pack, sling, cream).
- Kosa kata rumah sakit: prescription, chemist, ambulance, unconscious, rushed to hospital, come around, stitches.
- *Wound* (senjata, perang) vs *injury* (umum, mis. kecelakaan mobil).
