## Ringkasan detail

Video sesi 5 (sekitar 24 menit) dari English lesson UNSIA ini bertema *environment and nature*. Tujuan: menguasai makna kosa kata lingkungan dan memakainya sesuai konteks secara lisan maupun tulisan. Isi: fitur geografis bumi, natural resources (renewable dan non-renewable), masalah lingkungan, bencana alam dengan kata kerja pasangannya, solusi lingkungan, dan cara sederhana menyelamatkan bumi.

- [00:02–01:06] Pembukaan dan tujuan sesi 5. **Geographic features of the earth**: *mountain* (pegunungan), *river* (sungai), *lake* (danau), *desert* (gurun), *valley* (lembah), *island* (pulau), *ocean* (samudera/lautan), *glacier* (gletser, es di kutub utara dan selatan; dosen menyebutnya "gunung es").
- [01:06–04:12] **Natural resources** = materials found in nature and developed without the intervention of humans, digunakan makhluk hidup untuk memenuhi kebutuhan ("to meet their needs").
  - *Renewable resources*: can be replaced by nature again and again in our lifetime. Contoh: sun, soil, water, wind, plants, animals.
  - *Non-renewable resources*: cannot be replaced in our lifetime. Contoh: oil (fossil fuel), coal, natural gas, rock, minerals, metals (aluminium, tembaga, besi).
  - "Since we cannot replace non-renewable resources, we should conserve as much of them as possible." (*conserve* = menggunakan secara bijaksana/menghemat). Penggunaan sumber daya yang tidak bertanggung jawab menyebabkan masalah.
- [04:12–06:30] **Environmental problems**: *pollution* dari mobil, *the greenhouse effect*, *global warming*, kerusakan *ozone layer*, kualitas udara buruk. "When there is not much wind, a brown layer of smog hangs in the air." *Waste* (sampah), *pesticides and fertilizers* (pupuk anorganik) mencemari tanah. Greenhouse gases dihasilkan saat membakar fuels, terutama *coal* di pembangkit listrik untuk membuat listrik.
- [07:00–12:20] Audio hening di video aslinya (tanpa penjelasan).
- [12:24–16:29] **Natural disasters** (bisa terjadi alami atau akibat masalah lingkungan; climate change menyebabkan *flood* di satu tempat dan *drought* di tempat lain). Contoh kalimat:
  - *volcano*: "The volcano could erupt."
  - *earthquake*: "An earthquake hit the capital, causing panic among the population." (*hit* = menimpa)
  - *tsunami*: "No one knows the height of the tsunami caused by the eruption."
  - *drought*: "The area was constantly hit by drought."
  - *forest fire*: "The forest fire burned away for a week." (*burn away*)
  - *flood*: "The flood swept away many homes." (*sweep away* = menghanyutkan)
  - *storm*: thunderstorm, wind storm, snow storm, hail storm, sand storm. "The thunderstorm may settle the weather." (*settle* = menenangkan/menstabilkan)
  - *avalanche* (longsor salju): "Yesterday's avalanche killed a party of skiers and destroyed several trees." (*a party of skiers* = sekelompok pemain ski)
  - *sandstorm*: "To keep your bearings in a desert sandstorm is impossible." (*keep your bearings* = mengetahui posisi/arah kita)
  - *sinkhole*: "A sinkhole is a natural depression caused by the removal of underground soil by water."
- [17:29–21:35] **Environmental solutions**:
  - *Alternative forms of transport*: public transport lebih ramah lingkungan karena "buses and trains carry a large number of people at the same time"; *carpools* = "a way of reducing the number of cars on the road" (satu mobil dipakai bersama); mobil listrik juga disebut sebagai solusi lebih bersih.
  - *Renewable energy sources* (pengganti oil dan coal): solar energy (dari matahari), wind power (dari turbin), hydroelectric energy (dari sungai dan air laut); semuanya tidak mencemari lingkungan.
  - *Recycling* = using something again instead of throwing it away. "Glass, cans, paper and plastic can be recycled." Manfaat: saving energy and reducing damage to the environment.
  - *Green products*: recycled paper, wood from sustainable sources, organic fruit and vegetables (tidak memakai pupuk kimia).
- [21:35–23:36] **Save our planet** (cara sederhana):
  - "Turn off lights and other electrical equipment when not in use."
  - "Do not pour oil, fat, milk or chemicals down the sink."
  - "Use public transport or your bike instead of a car."
  - "Buy products that are recycled, reusable and reliable."
  - "Take shorter showers to save water."
  - "Write on both sides of paper."
  - "Use energy-efficient light bulbs."
  - "Apply waste management at home."
- [23:36–akhir] Dosen menyediakan tautan video untuk berlatih English, dan mengingatkan memperhatikan kosa kata environment/nature serta struktur kalimatnya, lalu menyebut referensi.

**Poin penting untuk ujian:**
- Fitur geografis: mountain, river, lake, desert, valley, island, ocean, glacier.
- Renewable (sun, soil, water, wind, plants, animals) vs non-renewable (oil, coal, natural gas, rock, minerals, metals); *conserve*.
- Masalah lingkungan: pollution, greenhouse effect, global warming, ozone layer, smog, waste, pesticides, fertilizers.
- Pasangan kata bencana alam: erupt, hit, swept away, burned away, avalanche killed, drought hit.
- Solusi: public transport, carpool, solar / wind / hydroelectric energy, recycling, green products.
- Perintah (imperative) untuk menyelamatkan bumi, mis. "Turn off lights when not in use."
