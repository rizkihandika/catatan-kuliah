## Slide 6

Cars | The biggest polluter today. Exhaust fumes are the main cause of bad air quality. When there is not much wind, a brown layer of smog hangs in the air. 

Pollution | Pollution is damage to the air, sea, river or land caused by chemical, waste, pesticides and fertilizer.

The green house effect | It is caused by harmful gasses known as greenhouse gases. These gases are produced when we burn fuels, especially coal burned in power stations to make electricity. Greenhouse effect causes global warming and climate change.

Global warming | It happens because the heat cannot escape the atmosphere, the earth is getting warmer. It may cause the ice in the North Pole and South pole to melt and sea level to rise.

Holes in the ozone layer
 | Ozone layer protects us from ultraviolet light from the sun which can have a harmful effect on animals and can cause skin cancer in human. If the layer is damaged 
by chemicals, hole appears and harmful light form the sun reaches earth.

The destruction of habitats | Wildlife is being threatened because habitat and woodlands 
are being destroyed. Rainforest are being cut down. Many species 
and animals have become extinct and many more are endangered.

## Slide 8

Alternative form of transport | Public transport is more environmentally friendly because buses and trains carry large number of people at the same time. Car pools are other way of reducing a number of cars on the road. Even cleaner solutions are electric cars and bicycle.

Renewable energy sources | Instead of using oil and coal, solar energy from the sun, wind power from the turbines, hydroelectric energy from river and sea water do not pollute the environment. 

Recycling | Recycling is when you use something again instead of throwing it away. Glass, can, paper and plastic can be recycled. Recycling save energy and reduce damage to environment. 

Green products | Examples of green products are recycled paper, 
wood from sustainable sources, 
and organic fruit and vegetables.

Environmental  Solutions

Turn off lights and other electrical equipment when not in use.

Write on both sides of paper.
