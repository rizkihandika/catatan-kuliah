## Slide 3

Subject  | Verb  | object

They  | discussed | the film for hours.

Did she  | answer  | the question?

Verb + object

## Slide 4

Subject  | Verb  | object | Question word

I  | told | them | where                    to find it

She  | asked | (us) | why                        we were late

He | showed | everyone | what                      to do

Verb + object + question word

Note: there is no direct object after explain: “He explained me what to do”.

## Slide 5

Subject  | Verb  | object | infinitive

She | asked | everyone | to leave.

They  | told | us | to wait outside.

The doctor | advised | him | to stay in bed.

I  | wanted | the others | to help us.

He | persuaded | me | to go to the party.

She  | warned | them | to be careful.

Verb + object + infinitive ( to V1)

## Slide 6

Subject  | Verb  | (that) clause

She | said | (that) it was good.

He | suggested | (that) we go together.

She  | mentioned | (that) the film was tonight.

Verb + ‘that’ clause

Note: “that-clause” has subject and verb

## Slide 7

Verb + (object) + preposition

I asked the taxi driver  for direction to the hotel.

They blamed me for the accident.
       > they said I was responsible for it. It was my fault)

She complained (to the manager) about the service.
       > she was not satisfied with it.

The manager apologized for the noise outside the restaurant. 
       > the manager said sorry.
