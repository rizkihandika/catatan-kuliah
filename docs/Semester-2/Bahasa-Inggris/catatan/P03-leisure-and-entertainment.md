# P03 — Leisure & Entertainment (Like / Love / Enjoy + V-ing)

Tanggal: 2026-11-03 · Sumber: [`Sesi 3_MKU Bahasa Inggris_Leisure and Entertainment.pptx.pdf`](../materi/Sesi%203_MKU%20Bahasa%20Inggris_Leisure%20and%20Entertainment.pptx.pdf), [`SAT Bahasa Inggris Sesi 3 (1).docx`](../materi/SAT%20Bahasa%20Inggris%20Sesi%203%20%281%29.docx)

## Ringkasan 3 kalimat
*Leisure* adalah waktu di luar kuliah, kerja, tidur, dan kewajiban, yang diisi dengan hobi, olahraga, atau hiburan. Sesi ini memberi kosakata jenis leisure, olahraga (tempat, alat, orang, skor), genre film, dan bahasa review film. Grammar-nya: **like/love + V-ing atau to V1**, sedangkan **enjoy + noun atau V-ing** (tidak pernah *to V1*).

## Konsep kunci

### 1. Lima jenis leisure (slide 4)
| Jenis | Contoh |
|-------|--------|
| Creative | painting, drawing, photography, writing (poetry/journaling) |
| Active | walking, yoga, dancing, hiking |
| Passive | watching TV/movies, listening to music, meditation |
| Social | calling a friend, texting, joining a religious/book club |
| Cognitive | reading, puzzles, sudoku, board games, googling topics |

### 2. Ball games (slide 5)
| Aspek | Kosakata |
|-------|----------|
| Alat | boots/trainers, **racket** (tenis), **bat** (baseball/tenis meja), **stick** (hoki), **clubs** (golf), **net** |
| Aksi | throw, catch, kick, **head**, pass, hit |
| Tempat | **pitch** (sepak bola, rugby), **court** (tenis, voli, basket), **course** (golf), **stadium** (seluruh area), **bleachers** (tribun tanpa atap) |
| Orang | **referee** (sepak bola, rugby), **umpire** (baseball, tenis), **spectators / the crowd** |
| Skor | beat, score, **a draw** (seri), **lead** (memimpin), win–won–winner, lose–lost–loser |

### 4. Film genre & bahasa review (slide 7–9)
Genre: animated/cartoon, horror, comedy, sci-fi, musical, romance, action, historical, documentary (non-fiksi), thriller (biasanya kriminal).

| Ungkapan | Arti |
|----------|------|
| brilliant / superb acting | akting luar biasa |
| very moving | sangat menyentuh |
| violent scenes | adegan kekerasan |
| awful / dreadful | sangat buruk |
| a slow film | membosankan |
| a gripping story | cerita seru |
| good fun | menyenangkan, tidak serius |
| a complex plot | alur rumit |
| enthralling | memikat sepenuhnya |
| cliffhanger | akhir menggantung, bikin penasaran |
| binge-watch | maraton nonton banyak episode |
| at the edge of one's seat | tegang & penasaran |
| light-hearted show | tontonan ringan |

### 5. Bicara soal hobi (slide 10–11)
- Tanya: *What do you like to do? What are your hobbies? What do you get up to at weekends?*
- Jawab: *In my free/spare time, I … · My hobbies include … · When I have free time, I … · As a way to relax, I …*

### 6. Grammar: like / love / enjoy (slide 12–15)
| Verb | + V-ing | + to V1 | + noun |
|------|---------|---------|--------|
| like | *I like going to the mall.* | *He likes to play soccer.* | *I like pizza.* |
| love | *I love traveling.* | *I love to read murder mysteries.* | *I love music.* |
| **enjoy** | *I enjoy watching horror movies.* | **✗ tidak boleh** | *We enjoyed the movie.* |

## Istilah penting
| Istilah | Arti |
|---------|------|
| Leisure | waktu luang |
| Spare time / free time | waktu senggang |
| Gerund | V-ing yang berfungsi sebagai kata benda |
| To-infinitive | to + V1 |
| Spectator | penonton |

## Sering keluar di ujian / mudah keliru
- ✗ *I enjoy to watch* → ✓ *I enjoy watching*.
- **referee** (sepak bola/rugby) vs **umpire** (tenis/baseball).
- **pitch / court / course** jangan tertukar (golf = course).
- *a draw* = seri; *lead* = sedang unggul.
