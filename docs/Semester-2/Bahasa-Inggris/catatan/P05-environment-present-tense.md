# P05 — Environment & Present Tenses

Tanggal: 2026-11-17 · Sumber: [`Sesi 5_MKU Bahasa Inggris_Environment.pptx`](../materi/Sesi%205_MKU%20Bahasa%20Inggris_Environment.pptx), [`Sesi 5_MKU Bahasa Inggris_Present Tense.pptx`](../materi/Sesi%205_MKU%20Bahasa%20Inggris_Present%20Tense.pptx), [`Reading Comprehension_Habitat Loss.docx`](../materi/Reading%20Comprehension_Habitat%20Loss.docx), transkrip video Environment & Present Tense

## Ringkasan 3 kalimat
Tema Environment memberi kosakata sumber daya alam (renewable vs non-renewable), masalah lingkungan, bencana alam, dan solusinya; bacaan *Habitat Loss* menjelaskan bagaimana kerusakan habitat membuat spesies terancam punah. Grammar-nya adalah **empat present tense**: simple (fakta/kebiasaan), continuous (sedang terjadi), perfect (selesai/pengalaman dengan kaitan ke sekarang), dan perfect continuous (menekankan durasi). Kunci membedakannya: **rumus + signal words**.

## Konsep kunci

### 1. Environment vocabulary
| Kelompok | Kosakata |
|----------|----------|
| Renewable resources | sun, soil, water, wind, plants, animals |
| Non-renewable | oil (fossil fuel), coal, natural gas, minerals → harus di-*conserve* |
| Masalah | pollution, smog/exhaust fumes, **greenhouse effect → global warming → climate change**, holes in the ozone layer, destruction of habitats (endangered → extinct) |
| Bencana | volcano (erupt), earthquake, tsunami, drought, forest fire, flood, storm, avalanche, sandstorm, sinkhole |
| Solusi | public transport, car pools, electric cars, renewable energy (solar, wind, hydroelectric), recycling, green products |

### 2. Reading: *Habitat Loss*
- Habitat rusak → populasi turun → **endangered** → **extinct**. Data IUCN: >35.500 spesies terancam.
- Terestrial: deforestation/clear-cutting, perkebunan monokultur (sawit, karet), kebakaran hutan, desertification.
- Akuatik: limbah toksik, pengeringan wetlands, overfishing, mangrove dibabat, coral bleaching.

### 3. Empat present tense (slide 5–14)
| Tense | Rumus | Pemakaian | Signal words |
|-------|-------|-----------|--------------|
| **Simple** | S + V1(s/es) · neg: do/does not + V1 | fakta umum, permanen, matematika, kebiasaan, berulang, keadaan mental, perasaan/indra, instruksi, headline, jadwal, klausa *when/if* | every day, usually, always |
| **Continuous** | S + am/is/are + V-ing | sedang terjadi, rencana dekat, situasi berubah | now, right now, at the moment, next Sunday (rencana) |
| **Perfect** | S + has/have + V3 | baru selesai, periode belum selesai, durasi sampai sekarang | just, already, yet, still, ever, since, for, till now |
| **Perfect continuous** | S + has/have + been + V-ing | menekankan **durasi** dari lampau sampai sekarang, kesal/jengkel | for two hours, since morning, how long, lately |

Contoh slide:
- Simple: *Chinese people eat rice.* · *One plus one equals two.* · *The train leaves at midnight.* · *Earthquake hits New Zealand.* · *When it snows, I like to ski.* (koma jika klausa *when* di depan)
- Continuous: *Julia is making a sponge cake right now.* · *The climate is getting hotter each year.* · *He is watching a movie with his friend next Sunday.*
- Perfect: *He has just cooked the meal.* · *She has drunk two cups of tea today.* · *We have lived in this house since 2015.*
- Perfect continuous: *He has been watching TV for two hours.* · *Why have you been disturbing me?* (kesal)

### 4. Negatif & tanya
| Tense | Negatif | Tanya |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Simple | *I do not like math.* · *He does not fall …* | *Do you like math?* · *Does she exercise daily?* |
| Continuous | *Julia is not making …* | *Is Julia making …?* |
| Perfect | *Julia has not made …* | *Has Julia made …?* |
| Perfect continuous | *She has not been planting …* | *Has she been planting for two hours?* |

### 5. since vs for
- **since** + titik waktu: *since 2015, since morning, since I went to school*.
- **for** + rentang waktu: *for six hours, for two days*.

| Istilah | Arti |
|---------|------|
| Renewable | dapat diperbarui |
| Endangered / extinct | terancam punah / punah |

## Sering keluar di ujian / mudah keliru
- Simple present orang ketiga tunggal **+s/es**: *She drinks*, *He does not drink* (setelah *does* kembali ke V1).
- *since* (titik waktu) vs *for* (lama waktu).
- Perfect = fokus **hasil/selesai** (*I have read the book*); perfect continuous = fokus **durasi** (*I have been reading for 2 hours*).
- Continuous bisa untuk **rencana** dekat, bukan hanya "sedang".
- Kata kerja keadaan (*know, like, think* dalam arti pendapat) biasanya tidak memakai continuous: ✓ *I think she is kind*.

## Latihan cepat
(1) Water ___ (boil) at 100°C. (2) Look! The kids ___ (play) in the rain. (3) I ___ (live) here since 2020. (4) She ___ (wait) for two hours — she's angry.
