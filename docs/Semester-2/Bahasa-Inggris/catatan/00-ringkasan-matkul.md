## P01 — Introduction
Sumber: `Sesi 1_ MKU Bahasa Inggris_ Introduction.pptx`, video "Easy English Conversations" (2,5 menit)

- **Memperkenalkan diri**: nama, umur (*I am 26 years old / I am 26*), asal & tempat tinggal (*I'm from Bandung, I come from Jakarta, I live in Surabaya*), hobi (*What do you like to do?*).
- **Memperkenalkan orang lain**: *This is my wife. His name's Tim. Her name's Sian.* Respons: *Nice/Lovely/Great to meet you (too).*
- **To be** menghubungkan subjek dengan keterangannya, yang bisa berupa:
  - **noun**: *They are our classmates.*
  - **adjective**: *Are you nervous?*
  - **adverb**: *We are in the same class.*
  - Bentuk lampau **was/were**: *I hope your summer holiday was great!*
- Weekly practice: video perkenalan diri 2 menit diunggah ke YouTube (sudah dikumpulkan).

## P02 — Work & Responsibility
Sumber: `Sesi 2_…Work & Responsibility.pptx`, video (22 menit)

- **8 kombinasi kata "work"**:

| Ungkapan | Arti / pemakaian | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| work **for** | + nama perusahaan spesifik | *I work for Unilever.* |
| work **in** | + jenis tempat (umum) | *I work in a bank.* |
| **leave for** work | waktu berangkat kerja | *I leave for work at 6.30 a.m.* |
| **go to** work | cara/kendaraan ke kantor | *I go to work by train.* |
| **get to / arrive at** work | tiba dan siap mulai kerja | *I get to work at 7 a.m.* |
| **at** work | sedang bekerja | *I'm usually at work from 9 a.m. until 5 p.m.* |
| **off** work | libur / tidak masuk | *a day off* |
| **out of** work | menganggur | *I've been out of work for six months.* |

- **Menyatakan tanggung jawab**:
  - *I **deal with** complaints from customers.*
  - *I'm **in charge of** …* (punya kendali/tanggung jawab atas sesuatu).
  - *I'm **responsible for** …* (punya otoritas sekaligus kewajiban mengurus).
- **Kata sifat untuk pekerjaan**:
  - (+) *satisfying, stimulating, fascinating, exciting*
  - (−) *dull, boring, uninteresting*
  - Rutin: *repetitive, routine*
  - Berat: *tiring, tough, hard, demanding*
- **Cara kerja**: *9-to-5 job*, *flexitime*, *work in shifts*, *commute*, *work from home*.
- **Grammar**:
  - **Action verbs** = tindakan (*I worked for…, I attended a training*).
  - **Linking verbs** = keadaan (*I am confident…, You seem thrilled…, That sounds like a beautiful job*).

## P03 — Leisure & Entertainment
Sumber: `Sesi 3_…Leisure and Entertainment.pptx.pdf`, video (24 menit), SAT Sesi 3

- **Leisure** = waktu luang di luar kuliah/kerja/tidur. Ada 5 jenis:
  - **Creative**: painting, drawing, photography, writing.
  - **Active**: walking, yoga, dancing, hiking.
  - **Passive**: watching TV, listening to music, meditation.
  - **Social**: call/text a friend, join a book club.
  - **Cognitive**: reading, puzzles, sudoku, board games.
- **Ball games**:
  - **Peralatan**: boots/trainers, racket (tenis), bat (baseball/tenis meja), stick (hoki), clubs (golf), net.
  - **Aksi**: throw, catch, kick, head, pass, hit.
  - **Tempat**: **pitch** (sepak bola/rugby), **court** (tenis/voli/basket), **course** (golf). **Stadium** = seluruh area, **bleachers** = tribun tanpa atap.
  - **Orang**: **referee** (sepak bola/rugby) vs **umpire** (baseball/tenis). Penonton = **spectators/the crowd**.
  - **Menang-kalah**: *beat, score, a draw (seri), lead (memimpin), win/won, lose/lost*. Contoh: *Brazil are leading Chile 2-1.*
- **Olahraga, tempat, dan perlengkapannya**:
  - Athletics: track.
  - Motor racing: circuit, crash helmet.
  - Swimming: pool, swimming costume/trunks.
  - Boxing: ring, gloves.
  - Ice hockey: rink, puck.
  - Skiing: ski slopes.
- **Genre film**: animated/cartoon, horror, comedy, sci-fi, musical, romance, action, historical, documentary, thriller.
- **Bahasa review film**:
  - *Brilliant/superb acting*, *very moving*, *violent scenes*, *awful/dreadful* (buruk), *a slow film* (membosankan), *a gripping story* (seru), *good fun*, *a complex plot*.
  - Ungkapan lain: *enthralling* (memikat), *cliffhanger* (bikin penasaran), *binge-watch*, *at the edge of one's seat*, *light-hearted show*.
- **Bicara soal hobi**:
  - Pertanyaan: *What do you like to do? What are your hobbies?*
  - Jawaban: *In my free/spare time…, My hobbies include…, As a way to relax I…*
- **Grammar**:
  - **like/love + V-ing** atau **+ to V1**: *I like going… / He likes to play…*
  - **enjoy + noun atau V-ing** (tidak memakai to V1): *I enjoy sunsets. I enjoy watching horror movies.*

## P04 — Health + Verb Patterns
Sumber: `Sesi 4_…Health (2).pptx`, `Sesi 4_…Verb Pattern.pptx`, video Health (24 menit), video Verb Patterns (14 menit), reading *Fitness Pill*

**Health**
- **Gejala umum**: *sneezing, coughing, a sore throat, blowing her nose, a temperature* (demam).
- **Penyakit umum**:
  - *a cold* (pilek, karena virus).
  - *(the) flu* (seperti pilek tapi ada demam tinggi).
  - *diarrhoea*.
  - *I feel sick* = mual/ingin muntah.
  - Dokter memberi **prescription** yang ditebus di **chemist** (apotek).
- **ache / hurt / pain / painful**:
  - **-ache** (noun) dipakai untuk *toothache, stomachache, backache, earache, headache*.
  - **ache** (verb) = sakit ringan tapi lama (*my feet were aching*).
  - **hurt** (verb) = sakit lebih kuat atau mendadak (*My throat hurts. Where does it hurt?*).
  - **pain** dipakai untuk bagian tubuh lain (*a pain in my chest*).
  - Kata sifatnya: **painful**. *Fillings* = tambal gigi.
- **Penyakit serius**: *lung cancer, heart attack, hepatitis, asthma*, dengan pola *suffer from…*
- **Cedera** (masalah → penyebab → akibat → solusi):
  - *cut her finger* → *bleeding* → *plaster*.
  - *twisted his ankle* → *bandage*.
  - *a bruise* → *swollen, black and blue* → *ice pack*.
  - *broke his arm* → *sling*.
  - *burnt her hand* → *cream*.
  - Catatan dari video: struktur Inggrisnya seolah-olah subjek melakukannya sendiri ("*He broke his arm*"), padahal kejadiannya tidak sengaja ("lengannya patah").
- **Rumah sakit**: *rushed to hospital*, *unconscious* ↔ *come round* (sadar lagi), *bumped into each other*, *ten stitches*.
  - **wound** = luka akibat senjata/perang (*soldiers were wounded*).
  - **injury** = luka biasa (*seriously injured in a car crash*).

**Verb Patterns**

| Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|
| V + object | *They discussed the film for hours.* |
| V + object + question word | *I told them where to find it. She asked (us) why we were late.* |
| V + object + **to V1** | *She asked everyone to leave. The doctor advised him to stay in bed.* (ask/tell/advise/want/persuade/warn) |
| V + (that) clause | *She said (that) it was good. He suggested (that) we go together.* (setelah *that* harus ada subjek + verb) |
| V + (object) + preposition | *I asked the driver for directions. They blamed me for the accident. She complained (to the manager) about the service. The manager apologized for the noise.* |

- ⚠️ **explain** tidak langsung diikuti objek orang: ✗ *He explained me what to do* → ✓ *He explained what to do (to me).*
- Preposisi harus pas dengan kata kerjanya, karena kalau diganti maknanya berubah (nanti nyambung ke materi *phrasal verbs*).

## P05 — Environment + Present Tense
Sumber: `Sesi 5_…Environment.pptx`, `Sesi 5_…Present Tense.pptx`, video Environment & Present Tense, reading *Habitat Loss*

**Environment**
- **Natural resources**:
  - **Renewable** (bisa diganti alam): sun, soil, water, wind, plants, animals.
  - **Non-renewable**: oil (fossil fuel), coal, natural gas, minerals, sehingga harus di-*conserve*.
- **Masalah lingkungan**:
  - **pollution**
  - **smog / exhaust fumes** (mobil = polutan terbesar)
  - **greenhouse effect → global warming → climate change** (es kutub mencair, permukaan laut naik)
  - **holes in the ozone layer** (UV, kanker kulit)
  - **destruction of habitats** (spesies *endangered/extinct*)
- **Bencana alam**: *volcano (erupt), earthquake, tsunami, drought, forest fire, flood, storm, avalanche, sandstorm, sinkhole*.
- **Solusi**: *public transport/car pools/electric cars, renewable energy (solar, wind, hydroelectric), recycling, green products*.
  - Kebiasaan sehari-hari: matikan lampu, mandi lebih singkat, tulis bolak-balik kertas, pakai lampu hemat energi.
- **Reading *Habitat Loss***:
  - Habitat rusak → populasi turun → *endangered* → *extinct*.
  - Data IUCN: >35.500 spesies terancam punah.
  - **Terestrial**: *deforestation / clear-cutting*, perkebunan monokultur (sawit, karet), kebakaran hutan akibat perubahan iklim, *desertification*.
  - **Akuatik**: limbah toksik, pengeringan *wetlands*, *overfishing*, mangrove dibabat, *coral bleaching*.

**Present Tenses**

| Tense | Rumus | Pemakaian | Signal words |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | S + V1(s/es) · *do/does not* | fakta, kebiasaan, jadwal, instruksi, headline, *when/if* clause | every day, usually |
| Continuous | S + am/is/are + V-ing | sedang terjadi, rencana dekat, situasi berubah | now, right now, at the moment |
| Perfect | S + has/have + V3 | baru selesai, periode belum selesai, pengalaman | just, already, yet, ever, since, for |
| Perfect Continuous | S + has/have + been + V-ing | menekankan durasi dari lampau sampai sekarang, kesal | for two hours, since morning, how long |

- Contoh:
  - *Chinese people eat rice.*
  - *The train leaves at midnight.*
  - *Julia is making a sponge cake right now.*
  - *We have lived in this house since 2015.*
  - *He has been watching TV for two hours.*
