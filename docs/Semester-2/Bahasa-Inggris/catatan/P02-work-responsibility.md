# P02 — Work & Responsibility (Action Verbs vs Linking Verbs)

Tanggal: 2026-10-27 · Sumber: [`Sesi 2_MKU Bahasa Inggris_Work & Responsibility.pptx`](../materi/Sesi%202_MKU%20Bahasa%20Inggris_Work%20%26%20Responsibility.pptx), [`SAT Bahasa Inggris Sesi 2.docx`](../materi/SAT%20Bahasa%20Inggris%20Sesi%202.docx), transkrip video Work & Responsibility (±22 menit)

## Ringkasan 3 kalimat
Sesi ini membangun kosakata dunia kerja: kombinasi kata *work* dengan preposisi, ungkapan tanggung jawab, kata sifat untuk pekerjaan, dan pola kerja. Grammar-nya membedakan **action verbs** (menyatakan tindakan) dan **linking verbs** (menghubungkan subjek dengan keadaannya). Target SAT: memakai kosakata kerja dan kata kerja dalam konteks *past* dan *present*, lisan maupun tulisan.

## Konsep kunci

### 1. Kombinasi kata "work" (video)
| Ungkapan | Pemakaian | Contoh |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| work **for** | + nama perusahaan | *I work for Unilever.* |
| work **in** | + jenis tempat/bidang | *I work in a bank.* |
| **leave for** work | waktu berangkat | *I leave for work at 6.30.* |
| **go to** work | cara/kendaraan | *I go to work by train.* |
| **get to / arrive at** work | tiba | *I get to work at 7.* |
| **at** work | sedang bekerja | *I'm at work from 9 to 5.* |
| **off** work | libur / tidak masuk | *I'm off work today.* · *a day off* |
| **out of** work | menganggur | *He's been out of work for six months.* |

### 2. Menyatakan tanggung jawab
- *I **deal with** customer complaints.* (menangani)
- *I'm **in charge of** the IT department.* (memegang kendali)
- *I'm **responsible for** the payroll.* (berwenang & berkewajiban)

### 3. Kata sifat & pola kerja
| Kelompok | Kata |
|----------|------|
| Positif | satisfying, stimulating, fascinating, exciting |
| Negatif | dull, boring, uninteresting |
| Rutin | repetitive, routine |
| Berat | tiring, tough, hard, demanding |
| Pola kerja | *9-to-5 job, flexitime, work in shifts, commute, work from home* |

### 4. Action verbs vs linking verbs (slide 25–27)
**Intuisi**: action verb = ada "gerakan/kegiatan"; linking verb = seperti tanda "=" antara subjek dan keadaannya.

| Jenis | Fungsi | Contoh slide |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| **Action** | menyatakan tindakan | *I **worked** for Peasant House Pub.* · *I **attended** a catering training.* · *I **was giving** him ideas.* |
| **Linking** | menghubungkan subjek dengan keadaan/sifat | *I **am** confident to learn new program.* · *You **seem** thrilled by the idea of working overtime.* · *That **sounds** like a beautiful job.* |

Linking verbs umum: *be (am/is/are/was/were), seem, become, look, sound, feel, taste, smell, appear, get* (dalam arti "menjadi").

**Tes cepat**: ganti verb-nya dengan *is/are/was*. Kalau makna tetap masuk akal → linking verb.
- *She looks tired* → *She is tired* ✓ → linking.
- *She looks at the screen* → ✗ → action (melihat).

## Istilah penting
| Istilah | Arti |
|---------|------|
| Commute | pulang-pergi kerja (perjalanan rutin) |
| Flexitime | jam kerja fleksibel |
| Work in shifts | kerja bergiliran (sif) |
| Overtime | lembur |
| Demanding | menuntut banyak tenaga/waktu |
| Out of work | menganggur |

## Sering keluar di ujian / mudah keliru
- *work **for*** + perusahaan, *work **in*** + bidang/tempat umum.
- *off work* (libur) ≠ *out of work* (menganggur).
- Kata kerja yang sama bisa action atau linking tergantung makna: *feel the fabric* (action) vs *feel happy* (linking).
- Setelah linking verb pakai **adjective**, bukan adverb: ✓ *That sounds good*, ✗ *That sounds well*.

## Latihan cepat
