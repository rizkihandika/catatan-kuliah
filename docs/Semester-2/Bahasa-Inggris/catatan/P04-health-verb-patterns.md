# P04 — Health & Verb Patterns

Tanggal: 2026-11-10 · Sumber: [`Sesi 4_MKU Bahasa Inggris_Health (2).pptx`](../materi/Sesi%204_MKU%20Bahasa%20Inggris_Health%20%282%29.pptx), [`Sesi 4_MKU Bahasa Inggris_Verb Pattern.pptx`](../materi/Sesi%204_MKU%20Bahasa%20Inggris_Verb%20Pattern.pptx), [`Reading comprehension_Fitness Pill.docx`](../materi/Reading%20comprehension_Fitness%20Pill.docx), transkrip video Health & Verb Patterns

## Ringkasan 3 kalimat
Tema Health memberi kosakata gejala, penyakit, rasa sakit (*ache/hurt/pain*), cedera beserta penanganannya, dan situasi rumah sakit. Grammar-nya adalah **lima verb patterns**: V + object, V + object + question word, V + object + to V1, V + (that) clause, dan V + (object) + preposition. Bacaan *Fitness Pill* melatih reading comprehension dengan kosakata kesehatan dan olahraga.

## Konsep kunci

### 1. Gejala & penyakit umum
- Gejala: *sneezing, coughing, a sore throat, blowing one's nose, a temperature* (demam).
- *a cold* (pilek) · *(the) flu* (pilek + demam tinggi) · *diarrhoea* · *I feel sick* (= mual).
- Dokter memberi **prescription** → ditebus di **chemist** (apotek).
- Penyakit serius: *lung cancer, heart attack, hepatitis, asthma* — pola *suffer from …*

### 2. ache / hurt / pain
| Kata | Pemakaian | Contoh |
|------|-----------|--------|
| **-ache** (noun) | 5 bagian tubuh tertentu | *toothache, stomachache, backache, earache, headache* |
| **ache** (verb) | sakit ringan tapi lama | *My feet were aching.* |
| **hurt** (verb) | sakit lebih kuat/mendadak | *My throat hurts. Where does it hurt?* |
| **pain** (noun) | bagian tubuh lain | *a pain in my chest* |
| **painful** (adj) | menyakitkan | *The injection was painful.* |

### 3. Cedera: masalah → akibat → penanganan
| Kejadian | Akibat | Penanganan |
|----------|--------|------------|
| *cut her finger* | bleeding | plaster |
| *twisted his ankle* | — | bandage |
| *a bruise* | swollen, black and blue | ice pack |
| *broke his arm* | — | sling |
| *burnt her hand* | — | cream |

Catatan video: bahasa Inggris memakai subjek orang meski tidak sengaja (*He broke his arm* = lengannya patah).

### 4. Rumah sakit
*rushed to hospital · unconscious ↔ come round (sadar kembali) · ten stitches (jahitan)*
- **wound** = luka karena senjata/perang (*soldiers were wounded*).
- **injury** = luka karena kecelakaan (*seriously injured in a car crash*).

### 5. Lima verb patterns (slide 3–7)
| Pola | Contoh |
|------|--------|
| V + object | *They discussed **the film** for hours.* · *Did she answer **the question**?* |
| V + object + question word | *I told **them** where to find it.* · *She asked **(us)** why we were late.* · *He showed **everyone** what to do.* |
| V + object + **to V1** | *She asked everyone **to leave**.* · *The doctor advised him **to stay** in bed.* · *He persuaded me **to go**.* · *She warned them **to be** careful.* |
| V + **(that) clause** | *She said (that) it was good.* · *He suggested (that) we go together.* · *She mentioned (that) the film was tonight.* |
| V + (object) + **preposition** | *I asked the driver **for** directions.* · *They blamed me **for** the accident.* · *She complained (to the manager) **about** the service.* · *The manager apologized **for** the noise.* |

- *that*-clause wajib punya **subjek + verb** sendiri.
- Verb pola "+ object + to V1": **ask, tell, advise, want, persuade, warn**.

### 6. Reading: *Fitness Pill*
- 1990-an: ilmuwan perusahaan obat menemukan zat yang memberi tikus manfaat seperti olahraga (otot, stamina, bakar lemak).
- Masalah: tikus berisiko **kanker** → obat tidak pernah **approved** untuk manusia.
- Peneliti masih mencari obat serupa yang aman (berguna bagi pasien yang tak bisa bangun dari ranjang, diabetes, penyakit pengecilan otot, dan orang dewasa yang tak punya waktu 40 menit olahraga harian).
- Dunia olahraga khawatir dipakai sebagai **performance-enhancing drug**; obat baru belum dites sampai otoritas mengetahuinya.

## Sering keluar di ujian / mudah keliru
- ✗ *He explained me what to do* → ✓ *He explained (to me) what to do* (**explain** tidak langsung diikuti objek orang).
- ✗ *He suggested me to go* → ✓ *He suggested (that) I go / we go*.
- *headache* (noun) vs *my head hurts* (verb) — jangan ✗ *my head aches me*.
- *wound* (senjata) vs *injury* (kecelakaan).
- Preposisi menempel ke verb-nya: blame **for**, complain **about**, apologize **for**, ask **for**.

## Latihan cepat
Perbaiki: (1) She advised me stay at home. (2) He apologized about the delay. (3) Can you explain me this?
