# P06 — Travel & Tourism & Past Tenses

Tanggal: 2026-11-27 · Sumber: [`Sesi 6_MKU Bahasa Inggris_Travel and Tourism.pptx`](../materi/Sesi%206_MKU%20Bahasa%20Inggris_Travel%20and%20Tourism.pptx), [`Sesi 6_MKU Bahasa Inggris_Past Tense.pptx`](../materi/Sesi%206_MKU%20Bahasa%20Inggris_Past%20Tense.pptx)

## Ringkasan 3 kalimat
Tema Travel & Tourism memberi kosakata perjalanan (trip/travel/journey/tourism, booking, fare, check in/out, stopover), ungkapan siap pakai di **hotel** dan **restoran**, serta *phrasal verbs* perjalanan (set off, take off, pick up, dst.). Grammar-nya adalah **empat past tense**: simple (kejadian selesai di masa lalu), continuous (sedang berlangsung di titik waktu lampau), perfect (kejadian yang lebih dulu sebelum kejadian lampau lain), dan perfect continuous (menekankan durasi sampai titik lampau). Kunci utama untuk dipakai sehari-hari: **simple past + V2**, terutama hafal *irregular verbs* dan ingat bahwa setelah *did/didn't* kata kerja kembali ke V1.

## Konsep kunci — Travel & Tourism

### 1. Trip vs travel vs journey vs tourism (slide 3)
| Kata | Jenis | Kapan dipakai | Contoh slide |
|------|-------|---------------|--------------|
| **Trip** | noun | keseluruhan kunjungan: berangkat, tinggal, pulang | *I'm going on a business trip next month.* · *Did you have a good trip?* |
| **Travel** | biasanya verb; noun hanya arti umum "bepergian" | kegiatan berpindah tempat secara umum | *We had been travelling all day and were exhausted.* · *Foreign travel is very popular among students.* |
| **Journey** | noun | perjalanan dari satu tempat ke tempat lain (panjang/pendek) | *The journey from New York to Los Angeles took several days.* |
| **Tourism** | noun | industri penyedia akomodasi, transportasi, layanan wisata | *Tourism developed in Central America more slowly than in the nearby regions …* |

Intuisi: *trip* = paket liburannya, *journey* = perjalanan di jalannya, *travel* = kegiatannya, *tourism* = bisnisnya. Jangan bilang ✗ *a travel* — pakai ✓ *a trip / a journey*.

## Konsep kunci — Past Tenses

### 6. Intuisi: kapan pakai past tense? (slide 3–4)
Bahasa Indonesia cukup menambah kata waktu: *"Kemarin aku **pergi**."* Kata kerjanya tidak berubah. Bahasa Inggris beda: **kata kerjanya sendiri yang berubah** untuk menandai "sudah lewat" — *I **go*** → *Yesterday I **went***.

Pakai **simple past** kalau kejadiannya:
- **sudah selesai** di masa lalu dan waktunya jelas/tersirat — *I stopped at a zebra crossing.*
- **berulang** di masa lalu (sekarang tidak lagi) — *We played tennis every day in August.*
- **keadaan** (state) di masa lalu — *We stayed at my grandparents' house last summer.*

Bedanya dengan present perfect (P05): *I **have lost** my key* (kuncinya masih hilang, ada kaitan ke sekarang) vs *I **lost** my key yesterday* (cerita masa lalu, waktunya disebut).

### 7. Rumus simple past (slide 5, 13)
| Bentuk | Rumus | Contoh |
|--------|-------|--------|
| (+) | S + **V2** | *Julia **made** a sponge cake.* · *He **went** to the hill station.* |
| (−) | S + **did not (didn't) + V1** | *Julia **did not make** a sponge cake.* · *He **did not go** …* |
| (?) | **Did** + S + **V1** …? | ***Did** Julia **make** a sponge cake?* · ***Did** he **go** …?* |
| *to be* (+) | S + **was/were** + … | *I **was** tired.* · *They **were** at the hotel.* |
| *to be* (−/?) | S + **was/were not** … · **Was/Were** + S …? | *The train **wasn't** late.* · ***Were** you at home?* |

Aturan emas: tanda "lampau" cukup muncul **sekali**. Kalau sudah ada *did*, kata kerjanya kembali ke V1.
*was* → I/he/she/it · *were* → you/we/they.

### 8. Regular vs irregular verbs
**Regular** = V1 + *-ed*. Aturan ejaan:
- umum: *play → played, stay → stayed, visit → visited*
- berakhiran *-e*: + *d* → *arrive → arrived, move → moved*
- konsonan + *y* → *-ied*: *carry → carried, study → studied* (tapi vokal + *y* tetap: *play → played*)
- 1 suku kata, KVK → konsonan akhir digandakan: *stop → stopped, drop → dropped*

Regular verbs yang muncul di slide: *stopped, carried, played, stayed, published, finished, arrived, called, escaped, died, moved, visited, rained, completed, walked, delayed, booked, checked, enjoyed, picked, dropped*.

Pengucapan *-ed*: /t/ setelah bunyi tak bersuara (*stopped, walked, checked*), /d/ setelah bunyi bersuara (*played, arrived, called*), /ɪd/ setelah t/d (*visited, completed, waited*).

**Irregular** = bentuk khusus, harus dihafal.

### 9. Time signals simple past (slide 4)
*yesterday, last night, last Monday / last week / last year, in 1998, an hour ago / two days ago, when I was a child, this morning (kalau pagi sudah lewat)*.
Posisi **ago** di belakang: ✓ *two days ago* (bukan ✗ *ago two days*). Tanpa *the*: ✓ *last night* (bukan ✗ *the last night*).

### 10. Tiga past tense lainnya (slide 6–12)
| Tense | Rumus | Pemakaian | Signal words |
|-------|-------|-----------|--------------|
| **Past continuous** | S + was/were + V-ing | sedang berlangsung saat kejadian lain/titik waktu lampau; berlangsung beberapa lama; berulang; perubahan bertahap | when, while, as long as, at 05:00 pm yesterday |
| **Past perfect** | S + had + V3 | kejadian yang **lebih dulu** sebelum kejadian lampau lain; sampai titik tertentu di masa lalu | by the time, before, when, already, still, just, ever, never |
| **Past perfect continuous** | S + had + been + V-ing | menekankan **durasi** sampai titik lampau | for, since, lately, how long |

Contoh slide:
- Continuous: *The children were doing their homework when I got home.* · *It was eight o'clock. I was writing a letter.* · *Her English was improving.* · *They were always quarrelling.*
  (−) *She was not eating a burger.* (?) *Was he traveling to Venice?*
- Perfect: *When the police arrived, the thief had escaped.* (= *The thief had escaped when the police arrived.*) · *I called his office but he'd already left.* · *They left before I'd spoken to them.* · *I'd never met anyone from California before I met Jim.*
  (−) *Chris had not completed the assignment …* (?) *Had Chris completed the assignment before he went to school?*
- Perfect continuous: *I had been learning French for two years before I went to college.* · *I had been working at the company for five years when I got the promotion.*

Intuisi garis waktu: **past perfect** = "masa lalunya masa lalu". Kejadian lebih awal → *had + V3*, kejadian sesudahnya → simple past.
Past continuous + simple past: aksi panjang (*was doing*) "dipotong" aksi pendek (*got*).

## Sering keluar di ujian / mudah keliru (kesalahan umum orang Indonesia)
- **Lupa mengubah verb** karena terbiasa bahasa Indonesia: ✗ *Yesterday I go to Bali.* → ✓ *Yesterday I went to Bali.*
- **Dobel past** setelah *did*: ✗ *Did you went …?* / ✗ *I didn't went.* → ✓ *Did you go?* / *I didn't go.*
- **was/were + V2** (mencampur to be dan verb): ✗ *I was went to the airport.* → ✓ *I went …* atau *I was going …*
- **Lupa past untuk to be**: ✗ *Last night I am sick.* → ✓ *Last night I was sick.* · ✗ *They was late.* → ✓ *They were late.*
- **Irregular di-regular-kan**: ✗ *buyed, goed, taked, catched* → ✓ *bought, went, took, caught*.
- **Ejaan -ed**: *stoped* ✗ → *stopped*; *studyed* ✗ → *studied*; *playied* ✗ → *played*.
- **Phrasal verb**: yang berubah hanya verb-nya: *take off → took off*, *get on → got on* (partikelnya tetap).
- *trip* vs *travel*: ✗ *How was your travel?* → ✓ *How was your trip?*
- Past perfect hanya perlu kalau ada **dua** kejadian lampau yang urutannya penting; untuk cerita biasa cukup simple past.

## Latihan
**A. Isian (bentuk kata kerja yang tepat)**
1. We ___ (book) a double room at the Palace Hotel last month.
2. The plane ___ (take) off an hour late yesterday.
3. ___ you ___ (enjoy) your trip to Bandung?
4. I ___ (not / bring) much luggage because I wanted to travel light.
5. She ___ (read) a novel in the departure lounge when the announcement ___ (come).
6. When we arrived at the restaurant, they ___ already ___ (close) the kitchen.

**B. Koreksi kalimat (perbaiki kesalahannya)**
7. Last weekend my family go to Jogja by train.
8. Did you checked out before noon?
9. I was lost my room key last night.
10. How was your travel to Singapore? Was there a stopover?

1. **booked** (regular, + *-ed*; *last month* = simple past)
2. **took** (irregular: take–took–taken)
3. **Did** you **enjoy** (setelah *did* kembali ke V1)
4. **did not / didn't bring**
5. **was reading** … **came** (aksi panjang dipotong aksi pendek)
6. **had** already **closed** (kejadian lebih dulu → past perfect)
7. … my family **went** to Jogja by train. (*last weekend* → V2)
8. Did you **check** out before noon? (setelah *did* → V1)
9. I **lost** my room key last night. (bukan *was* + V2; *I was lost* = "aku tersesat")
10. How was your **trip** to Singapore? Was there a stopover? (*travel* bukan noun hitung; pakai *trip*)
